# Licensed real-human exercise clips

These edited clips come from the [YMove free exercise video library](https://ymove.app/free-exercise-videos).

YMove permits the free clips to be used in commercial and non-commercial apps and websites, including edited clips, without required attribution. The source files must not be resold or redistributed as a standalone video pack. The copies here are resized, shortened, muted and packaged only as exercise demonstrations inside MyBody 2.0.

Some app exercises use a clearly disclosed equipment variation of the same movement pattern. The source exercise title is stored alongside each media record in `exercise-library.js`.
